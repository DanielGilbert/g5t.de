Ride Log

Keep a clear, private log of your vehicles and their maintenance.

Ride Log is a vehicle-maintenance tracker for iPhone. Record services, fuel-ups and mileage for every car, motorbike or bicycle you own, and let it remind you when the next oil change or inspection is due.

It's built around a simple idea: your data belongs to you. Everything lives in a single, human-readable Markdown file inside a folder you choose - your own iCloud Drive, the app's iCloud container, or just your device. There's no account, no server, and nothing is ever sent to me.

Features

  • Track cars, motorbikes and bicycles side by side
  • Log services, parts, costs, servicers and notes - with photos
  • Fuel & mileage logs with automatic fuel-economy figures (L/100 km, kWh/100 km or mpg)
  • Category-linked service reminders with local notifications - by distance, by time, whichever comes first
  • Quick odometer check-ins to keep mileage estimates sharp
  • Stored as a single Markdown file you can read, back up or edit anywhere
  • Private by design: no accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics
  • Available in English and German

Availability

Ride Log is free of charge.
